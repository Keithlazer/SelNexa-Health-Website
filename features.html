Offline-first health service management for African health institutions

Health institutions lose care continuity when power or connectivity fails. The SelNexa Health Platform keeps registration, records, procurement, billing, and reporting available in those conditions.

AFRICA CDC 5Cs

Technical alignment with the New Public Health Order

The SelNexa Health Platform reframes core features around the Africa CDC 5Cs pillars: Connectivity, Climate, Capacity, and Collaboration.

Connectivity (Intelligent Systems)

An offline-first, 2G-capable architecture keeps care records and workflows running where connectivity is unreliable.

Climate (Resilient Infrastructure)

Low-power design supports climate-resilient health systems, maintaining operations during climate-induced power and connectivity failures.

Capacity (Workforce Support)

Administration tools are designed to cut paperwork for staff in a region where WHO counts 1.55 doctors, nurses and midwives per 1,000 people (2018 data).

Collaboration

Our SDG 17 commitment centers respectful, action-oriented partnerships that prioritize domestic resource mobilization over aid dependency.

Platform Modules

These are capabilities of the SelNexa Health Platform, not separate company identities. Each module is designed for workflow efficiency, responsible data use, and deployment readiness in low-bandwidth care settings.

Administration

The Platform automates registration, scheduling, claims and reporting, with AI assistance for routine tasks such as suggesting appointment slots and flagging duplicate registrations. The aim is less coordination work for clinical teams and more time with patients, with every step kept auditable.

  • Offline-capable patient intake and queue flow
  • Scheduling and billing workflows tuned to local rules
  • Operational dashboards for administrators and implementation partners
Design target: reduce admin time per patient registration (pre-deployment)
Explore administration →

Predictive procurement

The Platform uses demand signals, seasonality, and facility history to help pharmacy teams prevent stock-outs without over-ordering medicines that may expire, turning procurement into a measurable health-system resilience workflow.

  • Forecast-guided reorder thresholds
  • Expiry and urgent-stock risk alerts
  • Procurement reviews designed for constrained budgets
Design target: reduce emergency reorder cycles and expiry exposure
Explore procurement →

Offline-capable electronic health records

The Platform keeps patient histories accessible at the point of care, records actions with tamper-evident audit trails, and syncs securely when networks return under local data governance rules.

  • Local data persistence during outages
  • Consent-aware access controls and auditability
  • FHIR-ready interoperability pathways for partner systems
Design target: preserve records continuity for low-bandwidth facilities
