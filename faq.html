FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about SelNexa Health

Answers about SelNexa Health and its flagship product, the SelNexa Health Platform.

How long does implementation take?

We don't have a measured figure yet; the first pilot will give us one. The timeline depends on the size of the institution, the records it already holds, and whether it uses the hosted deployment or the local network kit.

What about data security and privacy?

The SelNexa Health Platform encrypts data in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256), uses role-based access, keeps tamper-evident audit trails and respects patient consent when records are shared. Independent security testing is planned before the first pilot. The details, including what is still in progress, are on our Security page.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. The Platform is built offline first. Core workflows keep running without a connection, and changes sync when connectivity returns.

What's the pricing model?

We price per facility, based on its size and the modules it uses, not per user. Until the first deployment, every quote is scoped with the institution directly. For a quote, email sales@selnexahealth.com.

What training and support do you provide?

Staff training is part of onboarding. Support arrangements for a pilot, including hours and channels, are agreed with each institution before go-live.

Can it integrate with existing systems?

The Platform is designed to sit alongside national systems and exchange data with them through FHIR R4, including DHIS2 and OpenMRS, and Zimbabwe's Impilo and ePMS. Integration work for each deployment is scoped with you and your existing vendors.

Still have questions?

Our team is ready to help. Book a Platform demo or contact SelNexa Health directly.
