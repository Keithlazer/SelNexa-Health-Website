# Share button logos

The share buttons on blog posts use each company's own logo in its brand colour, as Keith's icon brief asks.

| File | Mark | Colour | Path taken from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| linkedin.svg | LinkedIn "in" logo | LinkedIn blue #0A66C2 | Simple Icons 12.4.0 (CC0). Later Simple Icons releases dropped LinkedIn at LinkedIn's request. |
| x.svg | X logo | Black #000000 | Simple Icons 16.37.0 (CC0) |
| whatsapp.svg | WhatsApp logo | WhatsApp green #25D366 | Simple Icons 16.37.0 (CC0) |

The marks are trademarks of their owners. They are used here only to label a link that shares the page on that service. Keep the colours and proportions as they are, and do not use the marks for anything else.

`tools/build-icons.mjs` puts them in the sprite as `brand-linkedin`, `brand-x` and `brand-whatsapp`.
