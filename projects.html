SELNEXA HEALTH PROJECT PORTFOLIO

One organization, multiple health systems initiatives

SelNexa Health builds, funds, and deploys practical healthcare systems for Africa. The SelNexa Health Platform is our first active product, and the portfolio now extends into youth health priorities aligned with the New Public Health Order, including mental health, maternal care, and community access.

PROJECT 04 · RESEARCH

Mental Health Detection

A youth-centered early detection and referral initiative designed to surface mental health risk earlier in care pathways. Evidence shows 50% of adult mental health disorders start by age 14 but often go untreated in Africa.

Expanded Youth Health Priorities

Non-Communicable Disease (NCD) Management

NCDs now account for 37% of all deaths in the African region, so future initiatives will prioritize prevention, tracking, and long-term care coordination for chronic conditions.

Vaccination Advocacy (AU Bingwa 2.0)

Our procurement module can support distribution of vaccines beyond COVID-19 by forecasting demand, coordinating stock visibility, and reducing vaccine-preventable illness across partner networks.

How We Decide What To Build

Infrastructure Fit

Projects must work under local power, connectivity, staffing, and governance realities.

Measurable Impact

Each initiative needs a clear baseline, pilot metric, and path to independent evaluation.

Sustainable Deployment

We prioritize systems that can be funded, maintained, and owned with African healthcare partners, combining public value with a credible revenue model.
