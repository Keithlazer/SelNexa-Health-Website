MENTAL HEALTH DETECTION · RESEARCH

Mental Health Detection

A youth-centered early detection and referral initiative designed to surface mental health risk earlier in care pathways. WHO estimates that half of all mental health conditions start by age 14, and that most go undetected and untreated.

SDG 3Mental health access SDG 17Referral and service partners

Role in the portfolio: Expands youth health leadership beyond SRHR into mental health detection and continuity.

Expanded Youth Health Priorities

Non-Communicable Disease (NCD) Management

NCDs caused 37% of deaths in the WHO African Region in 2019, up from 24% in 2000 (WHO). Future initiatives will prioritize prevention, tracking, and long-term care coordination for chronic conditions.

Vaccination Advocacy (AU Bingwa 2.0)

Our procurement module could support vaccine distribution beyond COVID-19 by forecasting demand and keeping stock visible across facilities.

How We Decide What To Build

Infrastructure Fit

Projects must work under local power, connectivity, staffing, and governance realities.

Measurable Impact

Each initiative needs a clear baseline, pilot metric, and path to independent evaluation.

Sustainable Deployment

We prioritize systems that can be funded, maintained, and owned with African healthcare partners, combining public value with a credible revenue model.
