Health Records Module For The SelNexa Health Platform

Secure, interoperable clinical records with offline access, consent controls, cross-facility continuity, and tamper-evident audit history.

Core Features

Tamper-Evident Records

Every access and update is logged for authenticity, traceability, and clinical accountability.

Encryption

TLS protects data in transit and AES-256 protects stored records and backups. Access is limited by role and by patient consent.

Interoperability

Records are aligned with FHIR R4, with integration pathways to DHIS2, ePMS, Impilo, OpenMRS and HL7-based systems.

Patient Consent Control

Patients grant/revoke access permissions in real time. Audit logs show exactly who accessed their data and when.

Key Benefits

Compliance-Ready Governance Controls

Audit-ready controls are designed to support regulated healthcare data workflows.

Resilient Data Continuity Design

Distributed integrity checks and resilient synchronization patterns protect continuity of care.

Designed to Reduce Duplicate Diagnostics

A single record that follows the patient is meant to cut repeated tests and keep treatment continuous across facilities.

Patient-Centered Consent Controls

Patients can see who has access to their record and change it.

Example scenario

Scenario: Interoperable Clinical Records

A hypothetical example of how the module is meant to work, not a case study.

Challenge: Fragmented records cause duplicate diagnostics, delayed care, and weak continuity during handoffs.

Platform response: Interoperable records workflows with cross-facility access and consent controls.

Target outcomes:

  • Improved records continuity during care transitions
  • Fewer duplicate diagnostics across facilities
  • Audit-ready access history and governance controls
