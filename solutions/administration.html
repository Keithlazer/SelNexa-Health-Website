Administration for Frontline Care Teams

Automate patient registration, scheduling, and record management with workflows designed for constrained healthcare environments. Staff time is scarce: WHO puts the African Region at 1.55 doctors, nurses and midwives per 1,000 people (2018 data).

Core Features

Automated Patient Registration

Intelligent forms that guide patients through registration, pre-populate records, and flag duplicates in real-time.

Smart Appointment Scheduling

AI suggests time slots, prevents double-booking and sends automatic reminders, which are designed to cut no-shows.

Intelligent Record Organization

Auto-categorize and file patient notes, lab results, and medical history. Full-text search for instant access.

Workflow Analytics

Track admin KPIs: registration time, appointment turnover, error rates. Identify bottlenecks and optimize processes.

Key Benefits

Designed for Faster Triage-to-Documentation

Workflow orchestration reduces handoff lag between intake and clinical documentation.

Designed to Free Staff Time

Automation is meant to cut repetitive manual tasks and rework, so clinicians and front-desk teams can spend more time on care.

Fewer Paper Fallbacks

Because intake and documentation keep working offline, staff shouldn't need to switch to paper during outages.

Operational Accuracy at Scale

Standardized workflows maintain compliance-ready records while supporting growth to multi-site operations.
