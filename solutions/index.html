Modules inside the SelNexa Health Platform

The SelNexa Health Platform is SelNexa Health's flagship offline-first health service management platform. Its modules cover patient administration, procurement, health records, analytics and a telemedicine beta for African health institutions.

Platform Modules

Choose the SelNexa Health Platform modules that fit your facility's needs.

Administration

Automate patient registration, scheduling and record management, with AI help on routine tasks. Designed to cut manual entry errors and free staff for patient care.

  • Automated patient registration
  • Intelligent appointment scheduling
  • Smart record organization

Design goal: less time between patient intake and documentation.

Learn More

Predictive Procurement

AI-driven inventory forecasting and supplier management, designed to prevent stock-outs, reduce waste and stretch budgets.

  • Predictive stock forecasting
  • Automated purchase ordering
  • Supplier collaboration portals

Design goal: fewer emergency stock requests.

Learn More

Health Records

Tamper-evident patient records, encrypted in transit and at rest, that stay available offline and sync when the connection returns.

  • Tamper-evident records
  • TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest
  • Offline access & sync

Built in: audit logs on every record access and change.

Learn More

Real-Time Analytics

Real-time dashboards and KPI tracking to spot bottlenecks, follow outcomes and support decisions with data.

  • Real-time dashboards
  • Customizable KPIs
  • Exportable reports

Design goal: earlier warning of bottlenecks in patient flow and stock.

Learn More

Telemedicine (Beta)

Low-bandwidth video consultations linked to the patient record, meant to bring specialist input to underserved areas.

  • Secure video consultations
  • Integrated scheduling
  • Low bandwidth fallback

Benefit: Access specialist care remotely.

Learn More

Built for Integration

Modules work together or on their own. Data exchange is aligned with FHIR R4, with integration pathways to HL7-based and legacy systems.
