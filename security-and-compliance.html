SECURITY AND DATA PROTECTION

Security and Compliance

How the SelNexa Health Platform protects patient data today, what is still in progress, and what has not been done yet. The Platform is pre-deployment: no health institution runs it in production, and none of the controls below has been independently audited.

Built into the Platform

Implemented and tested by our own team in development and in controlled testing. Independent security testing comes before the first pilot.

Security controls built into the Platform
Encryption in transitBuiltTLS on every external connection
Encryption at restBuiltAES-256 for stored clinical records and backups
Access controlBuiltRole-based access with least-privilege defaults
Audit trailsBuiltTamper-evident logs of account, record and policy events
ConsentBuiltConsent-aware access for patient-authorised record sharing
Data residencyBuiltConfigurable per deployment: in-country or regional hosting
Breach responseBuiltA breach workflow designed around SI 155 of 2024: notify the Data Protection Authority within 24 hours of becoming aware of a breach, and affected people within 72 hours where the risk to them is high

In progress and planned

We hold no security or compliance certification today. ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 work is control mapping, not an audit.

Compliance work in progress and planned
Zimbabwe data protectionIn progressData controller licensing and Data Protection Officer appointment under SI 155 of 2024, aligned with the Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]
Impact assessmentIn progressA formal Data Protection Impact Assessment for the first pilot
ISO/IEC 27001In progressMapping our controls to the standard. No certification has been sought yet.
SOC 2In progressMapping our controls to the trust services criteria. No SOC 2 report exists.
Cross-border transfersIn progressAdequate-protection checks and documented approval before any personal data leaves the country
Security testingPlannedIndependent penetration testing before the first pilot goes live
AccessibilityTargetWCAG 2.1 AA for the Platform's interfaces. Not yet independently audited.
Evidence for reviewersPlannedA pack of control evidence for procurement and due diligence teams, and compliance notes for each new country we enter
