WHERE SELNEXA BEGAN

Healthcare systems should not fail because the internet does.

In health institutions across Zimbabwe, we watched healthcare workers fight two battles at once: treating patients while working around power cuts, unstable connectivity, missing records, and medicine losses that should never have happened.

Essential stock expired unnoticed. Reports arrived late or not at all. Systems built for always-on infrastructure became unreliable the moment conditions became difficult - which is exactly when health institutions needed them most.

Most digital health platforms were designed for environments with stable electricity, fast internet, and constant technical support. Rural and district healthcare facilities were expected to adapt around those assumptions.

We chose the opposite approach.

SelNexa was built from the realities of African healthcare operations outward: offline-first, resilient under low connectivity, and designed to keep functioning when infrastructure does not.

We began as a professional collective of African health leaders and young professionals, and we still work that way. Young people are largely missing from health technology on this continent, a 'strategic absence' we set out to help fill by building the infrastructure ourselves.

Our Mission And Vision

Our Mission

Close Africa's healthcare infrastructure gap by building sustainable, locally governed systems that improve equitable access and continuity of care.

Our Thesis

African healthcare will not be strengthened by importing fragile tools built for stable infrastructure. SelNexa Health designs from local constraints outward: offline continuity, low bandwidth resilience, local governance, and practical implementation.

Our Values

Pragmatism: Build what can survive field conditions. Sovereignty: Keep data governance close to local health leaders. Partnership: Design with institutions, communities, and implementation teams. Impact: Measure whether each initiative improves care, operations, or access.

INCLUSION & EQUITY

Leave No One Behind

We adopt the UN and AU principle of Leave No One Behind as a design requirement for every deployment.

Inclusive Digital Access

The Platform is designed for marginalised young people, rural communities and people with disabilities, who are often left out by high-bandwidth tools built for other places. Offline-first workflows, low-bandwidth operation and accessibility-first interfaces keep care teams working across device and connectivity constraints.

Equity by design

We design with local health teams so that language, workflow and governance realities are respected, and to build systems communities can own and sustain.

OUR DEVELOPMENT ALIGNMENT

Why this work aligns with global health priorities

SelNexa Health translates global development priorities into deployable infrastructure. We focus on the practical layer that lets health institutions continue care, protect data, coordinate resources, and report outcomes in constrained environments.

Health systems strengthening

Offline-first workflows support universal health coverage by keeping patient access, records, and essential operations available during outages.

Infrastructure and responsible innovation

FHIR-ready interoperability, local data governance, and low-bandwidth design make digital health infrastructure useful where it is usually most fragile.

Partnership for scale

Deployment is designed for facilities, ministries, NGOs, research partners, and funders who need measurable outcomes rather than presentation-stage technology.
