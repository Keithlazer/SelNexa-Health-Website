Privacy Policy

How SelNexa Health handles the personal information you give us through this website. Last updated October 2026.

Information we collect

The details you type into our forms, such as your name, email address, phone number, organisation and message. This website does not collect or store medical records.

How we use it

To reply to you, arrange demos and follow up on your enquiry. We do not sell your data.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores a few settings for this site, such as your language choice. If you send a form while you are offline, your browser holds the request until it can be sent. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes all of it.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Please don't include patient details or other health information in website forms.

Applicable law and your rights

We handle personal data in line with Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]. You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing the address below.
