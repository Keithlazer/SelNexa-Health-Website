OUR MISSION

Co-architecting Africa's New Public Health Order

WHO's World Health Report 2006 found that the African Region carries 24% of the global disease burden with 3% of the world's health workers and less than 1% of world health spending. The gap between the care people need and the care they can get is not a knowledge problem. It's an infrastructure, access, and systems problem.

We started SelNexa Health in Harare in 2024 because the software on offer to health institutions assumed stable power and fast internet. Ours assumes neither.

Among other things, Africa CDC's New Public Health Order calls for stronger African public health institutions, more domestic resources for health, and respectful, action-oriented partnerships. We work in that frame. We're building a company that pays its way, because we don't think impact and revenue are in conflict.

Iterate fast, stay accountable

Healthcare crises do not pause for perfect products. We iterate fast while building strong, accountable institutions and implementation standards.

Institution-grade delivery · Field-tested iteration · Youth-led
