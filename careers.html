TALENT DEVELOPMENT HUB

Build healthcare infrastructure for 1.4 billion people

We are a small, ambitious team building practical solutions to Africa's most critical infrastructure gap. Our Talent Development Hub grows the next generation of health professionals and systems leaders.

Our Mission

Enable African healthcare providers to deliver faster, safer, and more resilient care through infrastructure-aware digital platforms.

Our Values

  • Pragmatism: Build what works in real clinical environments.
  • Sovereignty: Respect local ownership, governance, and context.
  • Impact: Prioritise outcomes that improve patient and clinician experience.

How We Work

Remote-friendly teams across Africa, cross-functional collaboration, and field-informed product development with healthcare partners.

Young Professionals Pipeline

Modeled after the Africa CDC Fellowship Program, this pipeline builds leadership capabilities through job-shadowing, internships, and mentorship.

Job-shadowing

Short placements with implementation, clinical operations, and data teams to expose candidates to real-world health system delivery.

Internships

Structured placements that build skills in product delivery, research, and health systems operations.

Mentorship

Guided development with senior practitioners focused on leadership, public accountability, and field-ready execution.

How we work

Field reality first

Product decisions start with facility constraints: power, bandwidth, staffing, procurement cycles, and patient flow.

Small teams, clear ownership

We keep decision loops tight and make each workstream accountable for outcomes, not activity.

Measure what matters

Every deployment needs a baseline, a monitoring plan, and a transparent evaluation pathway.

Build for sovereignty

We respect local data governance and design systems that health leaders can own and operate.
