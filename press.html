Company Boilerplate

SelNexa Health is a Zimbabwean software company building offline-first systems for health institutions. Its flagship product, the SelNexa Health Platform, is a health service management platform covering registration, records, procurement, billing and reporting. It is built to keep working through power cuts and network outages and to respect local data governance rules.

Key Facts

  • Founded: 2024
  • Headquarters: Harare, Zimbabwe
  • Flagship product: SelNexa Health Platform
  • Target markets: Zimbabwe, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa
  • Stage: pre-deployment. The first pilot is confirmed in Zimbabwe, pending ministry approval.
